# Governance

Swage currently uses a single-maintainer model.

- **Maintainer** — Abhik Sarkar (`@abhiksark`) has final say on scope,
  design, and merges. See `MAINTAINERS.md`.
- **Decisions** — significant design choices are recorded as ADRs in
  `docs/adr/`; architectural debates happen in RFC issues (use the RFC
  form) before implementation.
- **Becoming a maintainer** — sustained, high-quality contributions in an
  area (frontend, dialect, runtime, benchmarks) lead to commit rights by
  invitation; the bar is judgment, not volume.
- **Conduct** — `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` applies to all project spaces and is
  enforced by the maintainer.

This document will grow into a multi-maintainer model when the project
earns one; changing governance itself requires an ADR.
